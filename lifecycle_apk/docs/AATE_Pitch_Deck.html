The problem

Crypto trading bots execute clicks. They don't decide anything.

CLICK-DRIVEN

A human still chooses every trade

Telegram and web bots like Photon, BullX, Trojan and BonkBot make buying fast, but every entry, size and exit is still a person's call.

NO MEMORY

Nothing learns

Grid and DCA bots repeat fixed rules. They don't learn from their own closed trades or know when their edge has gone.

FLATTERING BACKTESTS

The results aren't real

Paper and backtest numbers usually leave out fees, price impact and failed exits, so they can't be trusted.

AATE02
The solution

An autonomous engine that has to prove itself before it takes control.

  • A native Android app with the whole engine on the phone. Your keys never leave it.
  • 16 traders across Solana memes, crypto alts, tokenized stocks, metals, commodities, forex and perps.
  • Decides, sizes, exits and learns from every close without anyone clicking.
  • One position ledger and one capital view, so the books always reconcile.
  • Paper fills pay real venue fees and price impact.

AATE04
The oracle

No probe trades. It admits or refuses, and it has to earn control.

The Predictive Entry Oracle reads up to 5,000 closed trades, per lane and across the whole book. It stays advisory until graded closes show its calls beat the alternative. It only takes control of admission once it has earned it, and hands control back if its edge fades.
