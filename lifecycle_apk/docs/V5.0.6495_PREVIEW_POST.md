## What just shipped in V5.0.6495

Four **source-level** fixes — bugs killed at the layer they were
born on, not patched by a downstream mirror.

1. **Paper trades were being registered as LIVE.**
   `ExecutorCanonicalMirror6442.mirrorBuyAttempt` /
   `mirrorBuyFill` had `paperMode=false` hard-coded. Fixed by
   forwarding the caller's `paperMode` through to
   `CanonicalPositionAuthority6441`. Paper session projections now
   route to `mode=paper`.

2. **Root cause classifier now wins display precedence.**
   `PipelineHealthCollector` now consults
   `RootCauseClassifier6471.classify()` and prepends its verdict —
   but only when the tier is `ECONOMIC_INTEGRITY` or
   `EXECUTION_FINALITY`. Provider-degradation labels can no longer
   mask a capital breach.

3. **`laneCap = 1.79e+308` sentinel gone.**
   `OrderSizeResolver6441` default replaced with a named constant
   `DEFAULT_LANE_RISK_CAP_SOL = 5.0`. The diagnostic no longer
   renders "$926M" figures.

4. **USD / SOL mark scaling repaired.**
   `BotService` mark-provider callback divides USD by `solPrice`
   before publishing to `CanonicalCapitalAuthority6450`. Equity
   stays SOL-denominated — the phantom $926M equity that
   appeared on a $50 wallet is structurally impossible.

Companion commit `919fb489a` also enforced provider circuit
breakers at every read site and quarantined impossible outcomes on
canonical event ingress.
