## Implemented

1. A live hard price/liquidity contradiction requires **same pool and <=5 s** alignment of on-chain and current market evidence. The ordinary 90 s chain cache remains available for historical depth/analytics, but is not hard proof of a live price collapse. A concentrated pool state quote now carries its pool identity.
2. A suspected contradiction with stale, missing or mismatched proof becomes `PRICE_CHAIN_REPRICE_PENDING_8847`, not a hard collapse. It triggers a short-age chain refresh, requests a fresh market mark when needed, and holds the same sealed ticket within the existing bounded ticket-retry budget. It does **not** bypass quote-impact, hard rug safety, position ownership, wallet accounting or attempt-spent protections. A contemporaneous true collapse is still a hard refusal. `Executor` no longer sends a chain-price wait to the unrelated curve-reserve retry system.
3. A proven origin witness is authoritative for selecting early launch phases and lifecycle age, even when the canonical birth hydrator has not stored its row.
4. A previously negative prediction does **not** permanently demote an accelerating coin: `CandidateAdmission8136.evidenceArrived8252` now promotes fresh, liquid, moving candidates for re-evaluation even when there is no held `DEFER_DATA` verdict. `MovingTokenRecheck8847` coalesces by mint (10 s), keeps at most 60 s of cycle priority, uses price momentum and (when actually observed) signed new buyers, and joins the existing `HunterWindow8293` slots. This is an observation/priority change, not FDG approval or a new entry gate; quarantined and banned tokens remain ineligible.
5. Regression tests prove 90 s old and cross-pool observations cannot serve as same-time collapse evidence; contemporaneous same-pool collapses remain refusals; reprice defers are retryable; witnessed births do not hydrate forever; movers are re-checked from new evidence without inventing buyer flow.
6. `TokenFacts.writePrice`, after ACCEPTING a real provider/chain observation, now advances the probation price watch. Never promote from archive/restore/cap-seed/phantom template. The first genuine observed price seeds a previously missing baseline, timestamp ordering stops late provider responses from resetting progress, and fresh +5% movement can reach the existing scanner/watchlist decision path without waiting for an arbitrary 30 seconds. This is **promotion for re-scoring only**; all V3/V4 and FDG entry checks remain.
7. The watchlist's LRU/cap and time/process-count stale policies now rotate cold candidates into the **existing probation tier** (with scanner and lane affinity preserved) instead of forgetting them; recent moving tokens are protected during the short reassessment window. A failed probation transfer restores the watched entry rather than silently losing the candidate.
