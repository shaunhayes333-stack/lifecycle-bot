# AATE P0 — native entry-zone intelligence (owner directive, 28 November 2026)

## Non-negotiable objective

Find the correct entry zones for every pocket, lane, trader, scanner/hunter and specialist setup, across supported meme, crypto, EVM, perpetuals, xStocks and other markets. The green zone is a learned viable formation for a particular setup, not one universal chart phase, not a global score floor and not a new decision gate.

For accumulation-driven setups, first demonstrate recognition of the confirmed green buy zone. Then study late accumulation as an earlier-entry challenger to maximise captured net return. Advance entry only where the earlier causal signal has demonstrated incremental after-cost expectancy and acceptable risk versus the confirmed formation. A lower nominal price alone is not superiority. Never force this shift on strategies that need breakout confirmation, continuation, reversal, volatility dislocation, liquidity events or other native triggers. For short strategies the target zone is the appropriate direction-specific entry, not an accumulation buy.

## Research and delivery contract

1. Bind evidence to full asset/market/venue, direction, native trader, lane, specialist, setup, source family, decision timestamp, feature availability, policy version and original risk/exit recipe. The current lane-only shared barrier model is a baseline, not a substitute for setup-specific evaluation. Do not silently pool structurally different plays or promote an unknown setup as a known one.
2. Preserve sequential snapshots before the move, including accumulation, ignition, retest/continuation, exhaustion and failed formation when relevant. Native strategy candidates may use different state definitions and horizons. Earlier zone is a causal hypothesis using only evidence received at that instant; no retroactive peak-derived inputs.
3. Compare viable entry alternatives in the same asset episode at reachable execution latency and intended notional, with independent chronological asset/episode holdouts, cost/slippage/route proof and adverse excursion, stop order, timeout, opportunity capture, managed exit and realised P&L. Include non-runners, failed formations, missed candidates, false early entries and opportunities lost by waiting. Account for correlated repeated observations, censoring and selection bias.
4. Per native setup, compare the incumbent, confirmed green-zone challenger and earlier precursor challenger where appropriate. Use risk-adjusted expected net return, calibrated target odds, drawdown, capital tie-up, coverage and after-cost matched results, not hit rate or marked peak alone. The shared learning priority does not require every strategy to pursue +30–200% or +500–1000% targets. Native markets and strategies need their own economic targets, holding horizon and trade direction.
5. Keep native V3/V4 and established protective exit/safety authority intact. Candidate probabilities are research until independent prospective validation, then scoped promotion through existing authority. No duplicate veto, universal buy condition, forced trade quota, position-size escalation or global earlier-entry switch.
6. Delivery: feature supply, zone/episode binding, timestamped ordered labels, per-setup walk-forward selection, actual execution, managed realised profit and missed-runner accountability. Distinguish paper, mark proxies, executable quotes and actual fills. Persist this directive across builds. No claim of market profitability from synthetic fixtures.
