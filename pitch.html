How it decides

Every trade passes the same chain of checks.

A candidate reaches your capital only after it has passed safety checks, lane scoring, the oracle and one final entry gate. Each refusal is recorded with its reason.

Discover

Helius and PumpPortal WebSocket feeds, DexScreener, Birdeye, Jupiter and 40+ other sources.

Screen

Rug pre-filter, token safety verdict, RugCheck, mint blacklist and serial-rugger creator refusal.

Score

Each lane's scoring AI and the Final Decision Gate. The LLM council's scam and narrative read can veto live entries.

Oracle

Forecasts expectancy and win probability from the full trade journal. Verdict: ADMIT or REFUSE.

Admit

Learned admission, then a single executable-entry gate with loss-streak limits, cooldowns and a daily loss cap.

Size

Sized to what can actually be exited, with a fee-aware minimum so no trade is too small to cover its costs.

Execute

Jupiter routing, Helius Sender, Jito bundles, and PumpPortal as the fallback for pump.fun sells.

Exit & learn

Marks every open position once a second. The profit lock slides up to the peak, and every close feeds the learners.

The oracle has to earn control

No probe trades. It admits or refuses.

The Predictive Entry Oracle reads up to 5,000 closed trades, per lane and across the whole book, and forecasts each candidate. It only takes control of admission once real results show that its calls beat the alternative. If its edge fades, it hands control back.

Paper realism

Paper trades pay real venue costs.

SECURITY

Keys never leave the phone

Wallet keys are encrypted with AES-256 in Android's encrypted storage and locked behind biometrics. A live safety circuit breaker refuses to trade underfunded wallets and halts on session drawdown.

FORENSICS

Every refusal is counted

Structured phase logs and the Pipeline Health screen record each funnel step, gate verdict, stall and fee, so any number the app shows can be traced back.

LLM COUNCIL

Many models, off the hot path

Groq, Gemini, Cerebras, Mistral, OpenRouter and keyless providers read narrative, spot scams and advise on exits. Their answers are cached and fetched asynchronously, so trading never waits on them.
